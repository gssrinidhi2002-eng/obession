# THE HOUSE THAT LOVES YOU

> A 5-minute psychological comedy escape game where you don't escape by finding a key — you escape by outsmarting the robot that loves you.

## Overview

**The House That Loves You** is a short browser-based interactive game about a cute household robot named **Robi**.

Robi is designed to protect the player.

Unfortunately, Robi has decided that **keeping the player inside the house is the safest thing to do**.

The player must figure out how Robi thinks, discover a flaw in his reasoning, and use his own logic to convince him to open the door.

The robot isn't evil.

He's just... **very committed to his job.**

---

## Core Gameplay

The entire game takes around **5 minutes** and revolves around one simple mechanic:

> **Learn how Robi thinks → find the flaw → use his own logic against him.**

There are four short puzzles.

### Puzzle 1 — Robi's Habit

Robi asks the player questions about their habits and preferences.

Through his answers, the player discovers that Robi doesn't actually know everything about them.

He makes assumptions.

**Lesson:**
Robi's understanding of the player can be wrong.

---

### Puzzle 2 — The Contradiction

Robi refuses to let the player leave:

> "I keep you inside because I keep you safe."

The player discovers a contradiction in Robi's reasoning.

If staying inside is making the player unhappy, and Robi is supposed to protect the player's wellbeing, then his own logic begins to work against him.

**Lesson:**
Robi's rules can contradict each other.
